# How to Try Share & Earn Lite

A short guide to the staging app, one section for each kind of user. The AI features are planned and are not in the app yet, so this guide covers the product as it works today.

**Staging app:** https://share-earn-lite-staging.web.app/

## Please read first

> **This is a shared sandbox.** Everyone who follows this guide uses the same test accounts, so data can change or reset at any time, and something you did may be undone by the next person.
>
> **Do not enter your own phone number or any real personal information.** Use only the test numbers below.
>
> No real rewards are paid out. Messages to WhatsApp open on your device but nothing is sent unless you send it.

## 1. The customer

Marcus visits Bloom Cafe, rates the visit, and recommends it to a friend.

1. Open the Bloom Cafe check in page: https://share-earn-lite-staging.web.app/b/brand-seed-02/l/loc-seed-02a
2. Enter Marcus's phone number and the code 123456.
3. Rate the visit. A high rating takes you to the share screen. A low rating at Bloom Cafe (2 stars or below) takes you to private feedback instead, with no reward.
4. On the share screen, pick a friend and confirm you have their agreement. Each friend is confirmed one at a time. A preview shows the exact message, including the reward note. WhatsApp opens to that friend.
5. Open your rewards to see what you have earned. Choose one, generate a one time code, and show it at the counter. The code lasts ten minutes once generated.

Watch it: [first visit](../assets/walkthroughs/01-first-visit.mp4), [private feedback](../assets/walkthroughs/02-private-feedback.mp4), [earn and redeem](../assets/walkthroughs/04-earn-and-redeem.mp4), [returning customer](../assets/walkthroughs/05-returning-customer.mp4).

## 2. The friend who gets a recommendation

James receives a personal link from Marcus. There is no open link anyone can forward.

1. The link opens a page showing who recommended the place and why.
2. James confirms his number with the code 123456 and creates an account.
3. James chooses how he wants to be notified.
4. At the café, James shows his code. Both James and Marcus earn a reward once the visit is confirmed.

Watch it: [a friend's recommendation](../assets/walkthroughs/03-friend-recommendation.mp4).

## 3. The business owner

Linda runs Bloom Cafe and sees who sends new customers.

1. Go to https://share-earn-lite-staging.web.app/business/signin
2. Enter Linda's phone number and the code 123456.
3. The dashboard shows visits, referrals, and your best advocates.
4. Open **Manage rewards** to see the rewards you offer and their limits.
5. Open the QR code page to see the code customers scan, and the staff briefing card to see what staff should say.
6. Open the feedback inbox to read private feedback. A customer who rated Bloom Cafe low in section 1 appears here, and the owner can follow up if the customer agreed to be contacted.
