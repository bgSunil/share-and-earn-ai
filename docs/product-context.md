# Product Context

What Share & Earn Lite is, so the AI features make sense. The main product code is private. This page describes it in plain language.

## The idea

Local businesses grow mostly through word of mouth, but a recommendation only works when it comes from someone you trust. Share & Earn Lite is built around that. A customer recommends a place to specific friends, not to everyone, and both people earn a reward when the friend visits.

## The journey

1. **Scan.** A customer scans a QR code at the business and signs in with their phone number.
2. **Rate.** They rate the visit and can add a short note.
3. **Share.** After a high rating they can recommend the place to named friends on WhatsApp. Each friend is chosen one at a time, and the customer confirms each one.
4. **Visit.** The friend opens a personal link, signs up, and visits the business.
5. **Reward.** Both people receive a reward, which the business confirms at the counter.

A low rating goes a different way. It becomes private feedback for the owner, who can follow up. No reward is attached to it.

## Choices that shape the product

- **No open links.** Recommendations go to named contacts only. A link anyone could forward would weaken the trust the whole idea depends on.
- **Consent for every friend.** The customer confirms each friend they share with, and the friend sees a clear consent screen before anything else.
- **Clear disclosure.** Every message says that both people get a reward.
- **Praise in public, comment in private.** Happy customers share. Unhappy customers speak privately to the owner, who gets a chance to fix the problem.

## Who uses it

- **Customers** who want to pass on a good find.
- **Friends** who receive a recommendation from someone they know.
- **Business owners** who run a café, salon, or similar small business and want customers to bring friends.
- **An admin** who reviews new businesses before they go live.

## Where it stands

A bootstrapped, early stage product. The staging version is working. It has not been piloted with real merchants yet.

## Where AI helps

Two moments have the most friction. A happy customer stares at an empty message box. An owner has no help replying to an unhappy customer. The AI features target those two moments. See [architecture.md](architecture.md) and [privacy-and-safety.md](privacy-and-safety.md).
